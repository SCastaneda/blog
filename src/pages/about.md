Sam — an Engineering Manager at [Bolt](https://bolt.eu), where I lead a team building Incentives across all verticals – Ridehailing, Food delivery, Car sharing, Micromobility.

I grew up in a small town in Germany, moved to Florida with my family at 16, studied Computer Science at the University of Central Florida, and have been building software ever since. A few years ago, my wife and I packed up our two kids and moved to Tallinn, Estonia — her home country — and I haven't looked back.

I started my career as a full-stack developer, eventually working my way into product development and then engineering management. I care a lot about developer experience, system reliability, and building teams where people actually want to work.

This site is where I write — sometimes about engineering, sometimes about life, sometimes about what it's like to raise a family across two continents. If something resonates, I'd love to hear from you.
