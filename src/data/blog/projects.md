Early in my career I built a bunch of small projects on the side — mostly to learn new things, sometimes to solve a problem, always for fun. Most of them are no longer running, but here's what they were.

## Filtch

Twitch doesn't let you filter a specific game's streams by title — Filtch does. The game is hard-coded in the config. I had it running for Magic: The Gathering at the time. My most recent personal project, written in Clojure.

[View on GitHub](https://github.com/SCastaneda)

## Photo Album

A weekly photo album I built for my wife and me to track how our son changed throughout his first year of life. It was the first project I ever shipped, written in PHP.

[View it live](http://photo.sam-the-man.com)

## Recipes App

Search for recipes by ingredients. Still technically running but not maintained — data isn't updated and some links may be broken. One of my first PHP projects.

[recipes.sam-the-man.com](http://recipes.sam-the-man.com)

## Hacker News History

Browse Hacker News stories by the hour — a time machine for HN. Written in Node.js. Not running anymore, but the code is on GitHub.

[View on GitHub](https://github.com/SCastaneda/hnhistory)

## Pathfinder

A digital version of the Pathfinder board game. Written in Node.js with a lobby, chat, and player-vs-player challenges via socket.io. This was a collaborative university project and was a lot of fun to build. Not running anymore.
